## 2. Deterministic discovery proxy (`ToolRegistry.search_tools`, top-5)

Lexical ranker over corpus prompts; an LLM would do better, so this is a
pessimistic reachability bound for the `agent_core + discovery` flow, not
a substitute for agent rollouts.

| set | n (tool cases) | recall@1 (primary) | recall@5 (acceptable) | primary in agent_core | core+discovery |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| all | 117 | 0.667 | 0.932 | 0.214 | 0.923 |
| development | 59 | 0.780 | 1.000 | 0.186 | 1.000 |
| held_out | 58 | 0.552 | 0.862 | 0.241 | 0.845 |

4 no-tool cases are excluded from recall (correct behavior is silence).

Reading:

- `agent_core` statically covers ~21% of case primaries by design — it is
  a front-door set, not a universe. Discovery recovers the rest:
  `core+discovery` reaches 0.923 overall on this lexical proxy.
- The development/held_out gap (1.000 vs 0.862 recall@5) shows authored
  keywords partly fit development phrasing. Scoring/keyword tuning used
  development cases only; held-out expectations were not edited.
- Held-out misses cluster where prompts use specialist vocabulary with no
  catalog overlap yet (e.g. bare version ranges, bare IPs, terse math).
  These are candidates for future keyword work, gated by real agent data.
